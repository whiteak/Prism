# Clipper

A phone app for making clips, sound bites and pictures from your own files, for Prism.

- **Clips:** trim to H.265 clips, sound bites (.m4a) or shrink whole videos; several parts at once or joined
  (straight cut, dip to black or crossfade); speed, turn, crop, fades and even volume; sound wave, play on repeat and smooth loops.
- **Pictures:** Phone, TV&PC and Icon presets (saved into their own Prism folders), crop, rotate, flip, resize,
  tweaks, text, undo/redo and several pictures at once; stills grabbed from videos land here.
- **Prism folder:** thumbnails and tap-to-preview, plus open, edit, rename and delete for what's in Download/Prism.

Built by `.github/workflows/clipper.yml` (dispatch, or a push with `[clipper]` in the commit message) and
released as `clipper-N`, never marked latest.
